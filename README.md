## Common questions

**Apps aren't blocking**
Make sure Screen Time permission is granted. Open Resist, go to Settings, and re-run Screen Time setup.

**I bought Resist but it's asking me to pay again**
Tap "Restore previous purchase" on the paywall screen. This recovers your purchase on any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

**How do I change which apps are blocked?**
Open Resist, go to the Dashboard, and edit your blocked apps list.

**How do I change the unlock duration?**
On the Dashboard, choose 5, 15, 30, or 60 minutes.
